================================================================================
                    LUNA ROBOTIC ARM & HAND - MASTER WIRING SCHEMATICS
================================================================================
This document provides the exhaustive, pin-by-pin electrical connections and 
power distribution architecture for the LUNA Robotic Arm & Hand system.

---
1. CORE HARDWARE COMPONENTS
---
- Host: Laptop with RTX 4060 GPU
- Microcontroller 1: Arduino Mega 2560 R3 (Motor Controller & Sub-system Hub)
- Microcontroller 2: ESP32 NodeMCU (Time-of-Flight Laser Array & Safety Hub)
- PWM Co-Processor 1: PCA9685 16-Channel 12-bit PWM Driver (Address: 0x40) [Arm Motors]
- PWM Co-Processor 2: PCA9685 16-Channel 12-bit PWM Driver (Address: 0x41) [Finger Motors]
- Actuators:
  - 1x DS3218 20kg-cm High-Torque Servo (Motor ID 2 - Shoulder Main Elevation)
  - 1x MG996R 12kg-cm Metal Gear Servo (Motor ID 3 - Elbow Joint Flexion)
  - 1x MG996R 12kg-cm Metal Gear Servo (Motor ID 4 - Wrist Roll & Pitch)
  - 5x SG90 9g Micro Servos (Motor IDs 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 - Hand Fingers Curling)
- Sensors:
  - 1x VL53L0X laser Time-of-Flight (ToF) Distance Sensor (Safety Perimeter Array)
- Power Infrastructure:
  - 1x 5V 10A DC Switch-Mode Power Supply (SMPS) [Actuators Power]
  - USB 5V Bus [Logic Power for Mega and ESP32]

---
2. POWER DISTRIBUTION & THE COMMON GROUND RULE (CRITICAL)
---
* CAUTION: Never power high-torque servos (MG996R, DS3218) directly from the Arduino Mega 5V pin! 
  Doing so will cause voltage brown-outs, corrupt memory, and potentially destroy the microcontroller.
* CRITICAL RULE: The Ground (GND) of the external 5V 10A power supply MUST be physically tied to 
  the Ground (GND) of the Arduino Mega and ESP32. Without this Common Ground, PWM reference signals 
  will float, causing servos to jitter violently and fail command execution.

Power Distribution Connections:
[External 5V 10A SMPS]
  - V+ (5V Output)  ---> Connected to PCA9685 Board 1 & Board 2 green terminal blocks [V+]
  - V- (GND Output) ---> Connected to PCA9685 Board 1 & Board 2 green terminal blocks [GND]
  - V- (GND Output) ---> Connected directly to Arduino Mega GND pin
  - V- (GND Output) ---> Connected directly to ESP32 GND pin

[Arduino Mega 2560]
  - USB Type-B Port ---> Connected directly to RTX 4060 Laptop USB Port (Logic Power & Serial)
  - 5V Pin          ---> Connected to PCA9685 Board 1 & Board 2 logic power pins [VCC]
  - GND Pin         ---> Connected to PCA9685 Board 1 & Board 2 logic ground pins [GND]

[ESP32 NodeMCU]
  - Micro-USB Port  ---> Connected directly to RTX 4060 Laptop USB Port (Logic Power & Serial)
  - 3.3V Pin        ---> Connected to VL53L0X sensor [VIN / VDD]
  - GND Pin         ---> Connected to VL53L0X sensor [GND]

---
3. BUS COMMUNICATION WIRING (I2C & SERIAL)
---
[Arduino Mega 2560 to PCA9685 Board 1 (Address 0x40 - Arm)]
  - Pin 20 (SDA)  ---> Connected to PCA9685 Board 1 logic pin [SDA]
  - Pin 21 (SCL)  ---> Connected to PCA9685 Board 1 logic pin [SCL]
  - 5V Pin        ---> Connected to PCA9685 Board 1 logic pin [VCC]
  - GND Pin       ---> Connected to PCA9685 Board 1 logic pin [GND]

[PCA9685 Board 1 (0x40) to PCA9685 Board 2 (0x41 - Hand) - Daisy Chain]
  - Board 1 [SDA] ---> Connected to Board 2 logic pin [SDA]
  - Board 1 [SCL] ---> Connected to Board 2 logic pin [SCL]
  - Board 1 [VCC] ---> Connected to Board 2 logic pin [VCC]
  - Board 1 [GND] ---> Connected to Board 2 logic pin [GND]
  * NOTE: To assign address 0x41 to Board 2, bridge the "A0" address pad on Board 2 with solder.

---
4. ACTUATOR CONTROL CHANNELS
---
All servos are plugged directly into the 3-pin headers (GND = Black/Brown, V+ = Red, PWM = Yellow/Orange) 
on their respective PCA9685 driver boards.

[PCA9685 Board 1 (Address 0x40 - Arm Control)]
  - Channel 0 ---> DS3218 High-Torque Servo [Motor ID 2 - Shoulder Elevation]
  - Channel 1 ---> MG996R Servo [Motor ID 3 - Elbow Flexion]
  - Channel 2 ---> MG996R Servo [Motor ID 4 - Wrist Roll]

[PCA9685 Board 2 (Address 0x41 - Hand Control)]
  - Channel 0 ---> SG90 Micro Servo [Motor ID 5 - Thumb Curl]
  - Channel 1 ---> SG90 Micro Servo [Motor ID 6 - Index Finger Curl]
  - Channel 2 ---> SG90 Micro Servo [Motor ID 7 - Middle Finger Curl]
  - Channel 3 ---> SG90 Micro Servo [Motor ID 8 - Ring Finger Curl]
  - Channel 4 ---> SG90 Micro Servo [Motor ID 9 - Little Finger Curl]

---
5. SAFETY SENSOR WIRING (VL53L0X Time-of-Flight Array)
---
The ESP32 reads the high-speed laser distance sensors over its dedicated I2C bus.

[ESP32 NodeMCU to VL53L0X Sensor]
  - Pin 3.3V (Out) ---> Connected to VL53L0X [VIN / VCC] (Power)
  - Pin GND        ---> Connected to VL53L0X [GND]       (Ground)
  - Pin GPIO 21    ---> Connected to VL53L0X [SDA]       (I2C Data)
  - Pin GPIO 22    ---> Connected to VL53L0X [SCL]       (I2C Clock)
